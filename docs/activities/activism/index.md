# Activism and Advocacy

>{: .note }
>> All the of Grand Research Challenge projects funded in the Reciprocity and Redistribution theme have activism and advocacy components, not only the ones exemplified here.

>{: .new }
>>### *Putting Knowledge Into Practice: MAMAS and Amplifying Mothers of Police Violence Survivors*
>>*This collaboration between Nadine Naber (University of Illinois Chicago) and [MAMAS](https://www.motheringisradical.com/) (Mothers Activating Movements for Abolition and Solidarity) works to overcome the problem whereby current prison reform and abolitionist frameworks do not value the trauma experienced by mothers of incarcerated individuals or realize the potential of mothers to create political change through advocacy and activism. They have focused on skill-building and knowledge sharing in order equip and empower mothers as they advocate their children-- in courtrooms, at the United Nations, and beyond. These efforts have led to the [overturned conviction](https://abc7chicago.com/post/antonio-porter-released-cook-county-jail-murder-conviction-overturned-connection-2022-chicago-shooting-dice-game/18488866/) of Antonio Porter and have led to creation of a participatory defense hub in Chicago that will focus on eqipping communities to lead the defense of their loved ones in court. Read more about the work and the social movement-led research framework that guides it [here](https://www.humanitieswithoutwalls.illinois.edu/news/reciprocity-redistribution-social-movement-led-research).*

>{: .new }
>>### *Putting Knowledge Into Practice: Environmental Justice Worldmaking*
>>*The [Environmental Justice Worldmaking (EJW)](https://sites.google.com/view/ejworldmaking/home) project centers ways that the humanities and humanistic social sciences can affect the just transformation of democracy. This project is a collaboration with Environmental Justice Health Alliance and Spelman College in Atlanta, and the Minnesota Environmental Justice Table and the University of Minnesota in Minneapolis. It aims to bring those communities most affected by climate crises in those cities into conversations about local solutions.*

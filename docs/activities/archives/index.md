>>## *DigitalArc Platform*
>>*[DigitalArc](https://digitalarcplatform.github.io/) is collective storytelling and community archiving platform for organizing, collecting, and publishing histories, with a central focus on community ownership and the people who contribute their stories. This [toolkit](https://digitalarcplatform.github.io/documentation/overview) documents the process of organizing an archiving event and creating a website to present the material in an online archive.*

>{: .new }
>>## *Putting Knowledge into Practice: Listening for the Long Haul*
>>*[Listening for the Long Haul](https://www.listeningforthelonghaul.org/) is an oral history and archiving project grounded in disability justice and focused on stories and lived long-COVID experiences. The project prioritizes slow listening and deep engagement, and centers the practice of **storylistening**, the twin of storytelling. This practice is particularly important in telling COVID stories, as it has been studied as a way to ease feelings loneliness and grief--hallmark charactistics of COVID experiences, in 2020 and in the present moment.*

>{: .new }
>>## *Putting Knowledge into Practice: Flint Water Crisis Public Archive*
>>*The [Flint Water Crisis Public Archive](https://fwcpublicarchive.lib.uiowa.edu/) aims to expand access to primary documents to illustrate how and why the Flint Water Crisis happened, through public records and community member contributions. This project has been working to organize the massive collection of emails sent by multiple government agencies into a searchable database in order to make the records of the ongoing crisis accessible to to anyone, but most importantly to those directly impacted.*

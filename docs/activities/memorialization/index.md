# Memorialization and Encapsulation

How do you encapsulate community engaged work? Some projects do this by design--archives, oral histories, and other storytelling and preservation endeavors. Other projects are more ephemeral in nature--workshops, events, and performances, for example. Methods of commemorating and memorializing are determined by the goals of the project. Is it something that will be replicated and repeated? If so, you may want to document your planning processes. Is it an event or performance that won't be repeated, but should be remembered? Then, you may want to tap into your community's creative and journalistic skills to photograph, video, and/or write about the event. Are you working with archival material to tell a story or present a particular history? You'll want to determine the best place for that archive to live so it can be accessed by the audience.

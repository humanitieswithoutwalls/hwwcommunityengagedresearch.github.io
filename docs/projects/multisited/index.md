# Multi-Sited Initatives

With strong, longstanding relationships comes an abundance of vision, ideas, and knowledge. This foundation makes it possible to carry out initiative across multiple sites, blossoming out of a shared and co-created vision and implemented in ways tailored to the priorities of the partners at each site. The Communiversities Grand Research Project is one example of a multi-sited initiative rooted in such relationships. When relationships are prioritized, the development of ideas, carrying out shared visions, and implementation of programming happens more naturally. This does not diminish the labor that goes into that implementation, but rather emphasizes the long duree of such work, because, yes, relationships take work. Cultivating and sustaining meaningful bonds takes effort from all involved parties. As the relationship grows and matures, the work evolves too and this evolution opens the doors for new shared experiences, one example being the initiatives proffered by the Communiversities project.

The Communiversities project takes place in three locations: Chicago, Cincinnati, and Detroit. Fundamental to the conceptualization of this project and the decision to apply for the Grand Research Challenge funding are the longstanding relationships between the three project leads. With more than a decade of experience working together and already established relationships with community partners in each of the three cities, the projects were confident in constructing a multi-sited initiative because so much of the groundwork was already in place. Building these relationships *and* carrying out the project activities at all three sites within the three year funding period period would have been impossible because these dynamics take time to cultivate and they can't be rushed. Because they were already concretized, the GRC funding made space and time for thinking and for more breadth to grow ideas. The goals of the project developed organically from the work that was already ongoing with these partners and in these places and the GRC funding was an opportunity to actualize it.

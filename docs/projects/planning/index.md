# Project Planning and Conceptualization

project scoping, planning, early stages, dvision of labor early on

How do you scope a project with the time and resources you have? Being realistic about can actually be accomplished and doing it well rather than trying to do *all the things* and getting lost in the weeds--especially in the early stages of a project, laying a solid foundation you can build on; start with big picture/vision casting and break it down into smaller, achievable action steps, figuring out what you need to accomplish each one (kind of a russian nesting doll of a bunch of small project housed inside the big idea); helpful for applying for further funding, having a "prototype" or pilot of sorts, being able to say "here's what we did with this little bit of money now pls give us more"

>{: .highlight }
One approach to project planning and defining roles is to use a Project Charter document. This downloadable template, developed by the Institute for Digital Arts and Humanities at Indiana University Bloomington, is easily adaptable for a variety of project types and can be used as a project management tool, ethical guide, and research document. Preview and download the project charter [here](https://tiny.cc/IDAHCharter).

# Sustaining Community Engaged Projects and Initiatives

applying for further funding and leveraging what you've accomplished so far 

advocating for university buy-in: figuring out what you need to keep going and who on your campus to ask for it (ex. course release and graduate assistant; ask dean), what campus partners can you bring in and how can cost sharing happen across units

building infrastructure that isn't dependent upon a single person (in the university or community)
